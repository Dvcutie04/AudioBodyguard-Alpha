# Audio Bodyguard live TV beta trial

Use this checklist with the installed candidate on iPhone or Android. Record the app build, phone model and OS, TV brand, exact model, TV software and firmware before each trial. Put the TV and phone on the same home Wi-Fi. Record a result for each TV actually tested.

1. Open **Devices**.
2. Open **Device help**.
3. Open **Illustrated setup guides**.
4. Choose the TV brand.
5. Choose the TV software shown on the TV if the app asks.
6. Follow one picture at a time. Mark each highlighted menu or button as a match or a mismatch.
7. At a mismatch, open **? Help**, then **My screen looks different**. Use **Back** to check that the preceding picture and progress return.
8. Check the result on the TV or in the official app using the table below.

| TV brand or platform | Physical check |
| --- | --- |
| Samsung | Follow SmartThings approval on the intended TV. Confirm it appears in SmartThings and responds to one remote command. |
| LG | Follow ThinQ registration. Confirm the intended TV responds to one ThinQ remote command. |
| Sony and supported Google TV or Android TV variants | Approve the code in Google TV. Confirm the app can move focus on the intended TV. |
| TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, Roku and Insignia Roku variants | Follow the Roku guide for the selected TV. Confirm the Roku app moves focus on that TV. |
| TCL, Hisense and Insignia Fire TV variants | Confirm the setup screen clears and the TV responds to its remote. Record any phone-app pairing separately. |
| Hisense VIDAA | Use the matching VIDAA guide. If testing an assistant link, confirm the intended TV responds to one supported command. |
| Philips Google TV voice remote | Confirm the remote appears paired in the TV’s Remotes & Accessories menu and moves focus. Use the Google TV check above for the separate phone-remote trial. |
| VIZIO | Confirm the intended TV responds in VIZIO Mobile after approval. For V505M-K09, separately compare the address guide and try the explicit network check. |
| Another brand or unfamiliar menu | Record the exact model and menu label. Record the guide as unmatched. |

Record the first mismatch or failure, the step number, and whether Back kept your place. Keep codes, passwords and private network details out of shared screenshots. Report external-app pairing, Audio Bodyguard reachability and guide completion as separate observations. Simulator and emulator runs establish software behavior only.
